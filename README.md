1. How did using Figma designs as references affect your coding process? 

Having knowledge of Figma designs, particularly the wireframing process, helped me visualize the layouts I needed to recreate the Frontend Mentor design challenge assignments. I approached each design by breaking it down into simple shapes, containers, and sections before starting to code. This made the overall development process much more seamless. 

2. What challenges did you encounter when aligning your code with the design specifications? 

One of the biggest learning outcomes from this experience was gaining more understanding of Flexbox and controlling the placement and alignment of elements within a layout. Recreating these designs and learning from instructions in class has significantly helped my knowledge over the past 3 weeks.  

A challenge I encountered was adapting the designs to different screen sizes and breakpoints to ensure they remained fully responsive. It required more effort and research. I plan to continue expanding my knowledge through further study and hands-on practice. 


3. How can the feedback and community resources on Frontend Mentor help you improve as a developer? 

Peer reviews and community feedback provide valuable opportunities to gain different perspectives on problem-solving. I view development as a continuous process of solving problems, so seeing how different developers approach the same challenge will enhance my understanding. Additionally, reviewing and providing feedback on other people's work is an effective way to reinforce my own knowledge, and my understanding of key concepts.
